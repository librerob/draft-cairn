# Ten-tester pilot

Status: prepared, not recruited. No messages have been sent and no applicant accounts have been created.

Recruit four independent grant writers, three agency staff and three consultants who currently fill long application forms. Use consented, manual outreach. Avoid asking for client documents, account access or answer contents.

## Invitation to send yourself

> I maintain Draft Cairn, a free browser extension that keeps local recovery copies of long application answers and helps reuse saved paragraphs. I’m looking for people who actually use grant portals to try a short synthetic recovery task, then optionally use it for two weeks where their data policy allows. Have you lost an answer recently, or do you usually draft somewhere else? I don’t need your client answers or login details.

## First interview, before demonstrating

1. Which actual portal and editor do you use? Give the vendor name or a public, non-confidential sample only.
2. When did you last lose work? What happened, and what did you do afterward?
3. Do you already draft in Word or another document? What still goes wrong?
4. Are local copies of these answers permitted under your organization's rules?
5. Do you reuse approved answers, and how do you check their length?

## Synthetic supervised task

Use the unmodified install ZIP in a fresh Chrome profile. Complete the native permission approve/deny/revoke checklist. Type only a made-up project description, wait for saved on this device, reload and recover. Repeat in a portal field the participant is authorized to use. Test an ordinary field, a rich editor and an embedded form if available. Choose a library answer, inspect its preview/fit, insert explicitly where supported, and verify retained text. Do not submit an application, bypass access controls or use real client data for the test.

## Two-week private tally

Record only counts and consented descriptions: protected sessions, recoveries needed/successful, missed fields, copy-only frustrations, approximate time saved and unwanted capture. Keep answers and confidential URLs out of reports. No telemetry is added to collect these automatically.
